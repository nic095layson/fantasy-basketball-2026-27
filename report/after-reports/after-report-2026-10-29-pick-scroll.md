# Trade and Free-Agent picks keep the list's place (owner, 2026-10-29)

Pull window: 2026-10-29 → 2026-10-29 (no pull: the 10-29 records on season/daily, read only, to build the page).

**Method up front.** Owner 2026-10-29: "when i select a player on either the Trade or Free Agent tab, the tool will automatically scroll back up to the top of the pool. Can you have it not do this, and stay on the selected player?" EVIDENCE marks what a run showed.

- Cause: each pick re-renders the card (`root.replaceChildren`), and each `.plist` box (230px, its own scroll) comes back new, at its top.
- Fix: `keepScroll(root, rebuild)` reads every list's scroll before the rebuild and restores it after; the window's scroll too.
- Evidence: a headless Chromium probe on the 2026-10-29 page built from season/daily, with and without the change (numbers in the table). Test: 1 new in test_season_deck, red first; season deck build PASS, 7 tabs, 0 page errors.
