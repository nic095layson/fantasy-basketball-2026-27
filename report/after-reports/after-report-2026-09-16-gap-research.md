## 2. The identity rulings

**Wagler vs Wallace — two different players, confirmed.** Every source spells
the rookie **Wagler** (the owner's "Wagner" reads as a typo): Yahoo's list,
NBA.com's player page, ESPN, Basketball-Reference (`wagleke01`), and the
Clippers' own draft announcement. He is a 19-year-old Illinois freshman
consensus second-team All-American (17.9 pts / 5.1 reb / 4.2 ast, 39.7% from
three, Big Ten Freshman of the Year), taken No. 5 overall by the Clippers
(EVIDENCE: clippers.com release + Yahoo + NBA.com + ESPN, searched 2026-09-16).
Keaton Wallace is a 27-year-old guard who has left the NBA entirely — §3. The
9/15 market analysis's name-collision flag is closed: no alias, two players,
and the pool side of the pair is the one that leaves.

**Allen Graves — identity resolved.** Toronto's No. 19 pick in the 2026 draft
out of Santa Clara (11.8 pts / 6.5 reb as a collegian), 6'9" three-and-D
forward with a Robert Covington comparison, strong Summer League showing
(23 points on 8-16 with 4-6 from three vs Miami) (EVIDENCE: SI Raptors + B/R +
TSN + NBA.com, searched 2026-09-16). Depth charts slot him on the second unit,
explicitly unofficial a month before the season. INFERENCE: ADP 98.4 against
expert rank 250 is Toronto-market rookie enthusiasm, not a signal our engine is
missing production — but a Covington-profile rookie (threes + stocks) is
exactly the shape our 9-cat engine values if minutes arrive, which earns him a
deep row rather than a skip.

## 4. The ADD tier — fit, role, opportunity

**Keaton Wagler (LAC, SG).** Multiple projections put the opening-night lineup
at Garland–Wagler–Ingram–Hachimura–Lopez (EVIDENCE: SI Clippers + Yahoo +
Yardbarker lineup projections, searched 2026-09-16); Summer League 18.0 pts on
33% from three; eighth-best Rookie of the Year odds. Fit: primary scoring
pedigree next to Garland's playmaking. Opportunity: high — a No. 5 pick on a
retooling roster gets runway. 9-cat read: PTS/3PM/AST with rookie-guard
efficiency risk (FG% drag likely year one). The owner's upside read is
supported. Demote if camp reporting moves him to the bench behind Kris Dunn or
Kobe Sanders.

**Deandre Ayton (WAS, C).** Acquired by Washington from the Lakers for Jaden
Hardy plus two second-round picks after the Lakers' Walker Kessler acquisition;
his $8.1M player option for 2026-27 was exercised (EVIDENCE: B/R + CBS + ESPN +
Arizona Sports, searched 2026-09-16). Fit: proven starting center on a young
roster. Opportunity: genuinely unresolved — the frontcourt also holds Anthony
Davis, Alex Sarr, and No. 1 pick AJ Dybantsa, and no source names a settled
starter. 9-cat read: FG%/REB with low-TO usage even in a timeshare. Demote if
Washington names Sarr the clear starting center in camp.

**Adem Bona (PHI, C).** 2026-27 salary fully guaranteed (~$2.3M); positioned as
the top backup center behind Embiid with Drummond gone to New York (EVIDENCE:
RotoWire + NBA.com + SI Sixers, with PhillyVoice corroborating locally,
searched 2026-09-16). Last season: 71 games, 17.4 mpg, 4.8/4.3 with **1.2
blocks** — elite per-minute stocks. Fit/opportunity: every Embiid absence
converts directly into Bona starts; the room's ADP 85.4 prices that contingency.
9-cat read: BLK/FG% specialist, near-zero usage cost. Demote if the Sixers add
a veteran center.

**Duncan Robinson (DET, F).** Started all 77 games he was healthy for last
season and led Detroit at 41% from three; favorite to start on the wing again,
though Detroit added four 40%+ shooters (Isaiah Joe, John Collins, Taurean
Prince, Gary Harris) (EVIDENCE: Yahoo + SI Pistons + ESPN, searched
2026-09-16). 9-cat read: elite 3PM, thin elsewhere — a late-round category
specialist our engine can price honestly. Demote if the crowd cuts him to
~24 mpg in camp reporting.

**Julian Champagnie (SAS, F).** Moved to the bench for Tobias Harris to open
2026-27, but re-signed on a three-year, $45M deal and is described as firmly in
the rotation — the movement shooting complements Wembanyama and the guards, and
he closes shooting-need games (EVIDENCE: Pounding The Rock + Yahoo + SI Spurs,
searched 2026-09-16). 9-cat read: 3PM with modest stocks; durable. Demote if
the Harris/Barnes wing crowd pushes him under ~20 mpg.

**Allen Graves (TOR, PF).** §2 — deep row on the Covington-shaped profile;
second-unit minutes to start.

**Aday Mara (OKC) — OPTIONAL.** No. 12 pick (Michigan, Big Ten DPOY: 12.1 pts,

behind Hartenstein, minutes projected "teens or lower" at full strength;
long-run Holmgren partner (EVIDENCE: Yahoo + CBS + SI Thunder, searched
2026-09-16). Redraft-thin, keeper-interesting.

**Al Horford (GSW) — SKIP.** Back on two years/$14M at age 40 as the backup

Warriors, searched 2026-09-16). The room's 86.6 is name-brand drafting.

**T.J. McConnell (IND) — SKIP.** With Haliburton back from the Achilles,
McConnell returns to primary backup point guard (EVIDENCE: ESPN + SI Pacers +
CBS, searched 2026-09-16). The room's 93.5 prices last season's emergency
role; the opportunity is gone. Fade confirmed.

**Luke Kornet (SAS) — SKIP.** Four-year deal, primary reliever behind
Wembanyama with some dual-big looks (EVIDENCE: ESPN + RotoWire + Last Word,
searched 2026-09-16). Real NBA role, capped fantasy ceiling.

**Johni Broome (LAC) — SKIP.** Sent from Philadelphia to the Clippers in July
for cash and a 2027 second (EVIDENCE: CBS + SI, searched 2026-09-16); Summer
League 14.7/9.0, but Brook Lopez starts and Niederhauser returns from foot
recovery — third-center path. The room's 105.4 against expert rank 668 is the
list's most inflated price.
