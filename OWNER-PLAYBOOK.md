# Owner playbook

How the owner runs his team, in his own words, so every session starts knowing it. Quotes are verbatim with the date
said. Lines marked *(session reading)* are a session's interpretation, not the owner's statement, and give way to his
word. This file records preferences and method; it authorizes nothing. A roster move, a trade evaluation, a projection
or a constant still needs the owner's pick (RULES.md, CLAUDE.md).

Add to it only what the owner has said. Change a quoted line only when he restates it; keep the old line with its date
struck through, so the history reads.

## 1. Roster room for injured players: the 2-for-1

> "Once my injured players are nearing return, I try and package 2 players to trade for 1, netting a better player and
> opening a spot for the injured player to return." (2026-11-11)

> "I usually trade the two players I view as least contributing to my categorical strengths. I also target opponent
> teams who are hit with heavy injuries and they are looking for multiple pieces to fill out their roster." (2026-11-11)

- The partner's wants change case by case: "It changes from situation to situation." (2026-11-11)
- What gets a yes: "It is a little of both [names and categories], but they are more inclined to accept if it is a
  bigger name player (Durant ex)." (2026-11-12)
- Standing veto, 2026-10-20: no compute on hypothetical trades. The simulator and the Trade Analyzer evaluate only a
  trade the owner has composed by hand. A session may screen targets from board values and must say that is all it did.

## 2. Free-agent budget (FAAB)

## 6. How the owner wants to work

- "I want this working session to be dialogue between the two of us. You are my Co-GM! Educate me and help me advance
  my fantasy comprehension as well." (2026-11-11)
- "I have gone against your recommendations at times, but evidence has shown it would've paid off." (2026-11-11)
  *(session reading)* A recommendation is argued with evidence and then it is his call; a session does not repeat a
  recommendation he has declined unless the evidence has changed, and says what changed.
- Positional balance is judged with the deck's positional counter, not by impression (2026-11-11; the session had
  called the roster guard-heavy and the counter showed 7 guards, 8 forwards, 4 centers).

## 7. Upkeep (owner's card, 2026-11-12: "Weekly review + playbook")

Each Monday a read-only review reads the week's grades, health checks and `report/corrections-log.md`, and reports to
the owner: what was wrong, what it cost, and fixes as options. It changes nothing. New owner statements heard during
the week are proposed as additions to this file in that report and enter it through a pull request he merges.
